About Farma

Demo Edition, Contact & Suggestions

Welcome to Farma Marketplace 🌱

Farma is an intuitive, direct-from-farm web marketplace designed for local growers, farmers, and families to buy and sell fresh produce freely and transparently.

🧒

Child-Friendly & Accessible

Created with bright icons, visual food emojis, and large stepper counters so that anyone—including small children—can easily learn commerce and navigation.

🛒

Two Distinct Pages

A dedicated Buy Page for shoppers and a Sell Page with a seller studio and real-time live stock management.

🛡️

Fair Trading Guard

Items you list are visible to everyone on the marketplace, but the built-in system prevents sellers from accidentally purchasing their own listings.

⏯️

Master Start / Stop

Parents and administrators can easily pause or resume all marketplace transactions at any time with a single tap.
